About The Flavor Vault

Your personal kitchen companion for managing and discovering recipes.

The Flavor Vault was created to help home cooks organize their favorite dishes. We believe that keeping track of your best recipes should be easy, secure, and always accessible.

Features

  • My Vault: Securely store your private recipes using Firebase Firestore.
  • Community: Share and explore recipes publicly with others.
  • Responsive Design: Access your recipes easily on any device, from phone to desktop.

Our Mission

a small community where users can save their culinary creations and find new inspiration from others.
